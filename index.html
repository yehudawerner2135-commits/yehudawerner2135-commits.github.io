Nechama's Salon app

A private booking app used by the staff of Nechama's Salon, a wig salon in Lakewood, New Jersey, to schedule appointments, track wig drop-offs and repairs, and send appointment reminders to clients who agreed to receive them.

Who uses it: only the salon's owner and staff, each with their own login. Clients do not sign in.

Google Calendar: if the salon owner connects Google Calendar, the app creates one calendar in the owner's Google account and keeps it up to date with the salon's appointments. It does not read or change any other calendar.
